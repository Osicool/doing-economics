## Learning objectives

- Calculate growth rates from GDP per capita.
- Test for absolute and conditional convergence.

## Tasks

1. Compute average annual growth, 1980–2019.
2. Regress growth on initial log GDP per capita.
3. Add investment and schooling as controls.

## Interpretation

Is there evidence of convergence? Which assumptions are doing the work?
