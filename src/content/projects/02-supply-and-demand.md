## Learning objectives

- Explain why OLS of quantity on price fails to identify demand.
- Use an instrument to estimate a demand elasticity.

## Tasks

1. Plot price against quantity and describe the pattern.
2. Run a log-log OLS regression.
3. Re-estimate with weather as an instrument for supply.

## Interpretation

Compare the OLS and IV elasticities. What does the difference tell you?
